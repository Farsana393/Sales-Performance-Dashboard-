# Sales-Performance-Dashboard

Show where sales come from and where the business should grow. 

# What I built

KPI cards (total sales, profit, cost, margin, quantity), a monthly trend, zone, customer-type and product-type breakdowns, a year funnel, a market map and slicers for month, year, zone and customer type.

# Key findings
April was the peak month. Sales were 4.74M in total with an 11.4% profit margin.
The North zone led with about 3.56M, while South contributed about 0.51M.
Brick and mortar made up 85.77% of sales. E-commerce made up 14.23%.
Delhi NCR was the strongest market. Recommended: expand in weaker regions and grow e-commerce

# Tools and Skills

- Power BI: DAX measures, slicers, page navigation, funnel, donut and line charts
- Sales trend, seasonality and market analysis
- KPI design, data storytelling and business recommendations
